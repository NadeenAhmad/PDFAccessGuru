The first public version of PDFAccessGuru.

**PDFAccessGuru makes PDF files accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.** It runs on your own computer, in your web browser, and never changes your original file.

What it does:
- **Tags** files that have none: headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, pictures, links and the reading order. Headers, footers and page numbers become decoration.
- **Writes descriptions** for pictures, formulas, links and form fields, with an AI if you like: on your computer (Ollama) or online (like ChatGPT). Without an AI it uses the caption or the text in the picture.
- **Sets the language and the title**, also for passages in another language.
- **Repairs** fonts, lists, tables, headings, links, comments and form fields, and checks every fix against PDF/UA with veraPDF.
- **Lists what a person should check**, and lets you correct it in the app: picture and link descriptions, the title, the language, and what was hidden as decoration.
- **Colour contrast:** always measured, changed only if you switch it on.
- **Report** in plain words, in the language of the PDF.
- **Several files at once**, all in one ZIP file.

Install: see the [README](https://github.com/NadeenAhmad/PDFAccessGuru#install).
