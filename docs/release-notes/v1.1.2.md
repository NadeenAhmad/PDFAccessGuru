Easier AI set-up and safer updates. Fixing PDF files works exactly as in 1.1.0.

- **Fields open when you choose an option.** Under **Choose who writes the descriptions**, the model list opens when you choose the AI on your computer, and the key field and model field open when you choose an online AI. You can now choose the online AI before you have saved a key; if you start without a key, the app asks for it.
- **The guided tour** explains where to choose the model and paste the key.
- **Updates that work on more computers.** **Update now** works with the newest uv (0.13), also finds uv when it was installed with Homebrew or cargo, and if uv is missing it runs the installer again instead of failing. If an update still does not arrive, the app says so when it opens again and shows the line to paste into Terminal or PowerShell.
