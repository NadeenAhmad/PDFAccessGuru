A screen reader reads a picture's description aloud instead of the picture. You will see 40 pictures from scientific papers. Each has up to three descriptions, written by different methods. They are in a random order and do not say who wrote them, so you can judge them fairly.

  2. For each description, answer: is everything in it true? How helpful is it for someone who cannot see the picture? Any problems?
  3. Judge each description on its own; two descriptions may get the same answers.
  4. Please rate on your own and don't discuss the pictures with the other rater until you are both done.
