# Sample of the rating site

The site the two judges used for the expert judgement, exactly as they saw it, with 6 files of the Kumar benchmark
(CC0) instead of all items:

- **Part 1, pictures** (`index.html`): 6 of the 40 pictures, each with its three descriptions.
- **Part 2, structure** (`structure.html`): the 2 Kumar documents of the PAVE comparison, each in the versions of
  Acrobat Pro, opendataloader-pdf, PAVE 2.0 and PDFAccessGuru.
- **Part 3, links, titles and language** (`links.html`): 10 links, the titles of 4 documents and 3 passages.

The versions are blind, as for the judges: letters in a random order. Which tool made which version is in
[`../results/expert-judgement/`](../results/expert-judgement).

**To open it:** download this folder and open `index.html` in a browser. The start page asks for a name; any name
works. Answers stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere. The site said "40 pictures" and gave times for the full
rating; the yellow line at the top is the only addition.
